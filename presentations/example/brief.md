# Presentation brief — fictional test fixture

**Client:** Northstar Manufacturing Lab (fictional). **Project:** Adaptive Production Monitoring Pilot (fictional).

Prepare a 12–15 minute review for manufacturing engineering, operations leads, automation specialists, and a small investment committee. Explain what the pilot tried, what the measured observations do and do not support, and what evidence is needed before extending it to an adjacent line. The decision sought is approval for a gated eight-week validation, not a production-wide rollout.

Aim for about 9–11 slides. Use a professional technical level: show the physical line and signal path clearly, keep operational measures legible, and make the advisory-only control boundary explicit. Give the matched baseline/pilot comparison and operator experience enough weight to inform the decision. Include limitations and unresolved questions without letting them consume the whole presentation.

The source files are independent inputs and should be synthesized. Prefer editable diagrams and data displays. Inspect the optional assets and use only those that help the story. All names, dates, observations, and quotes in this fixture are invented; do not imply a real deployment or causal proof.
