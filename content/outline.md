# Example deck outline

## Audience and purpose

Technical and academic audiences. Demonstrate a concise path from a research question to an actionable decision.

## Narrative

1. **From question to evidence** — introduce a practical framework for clear technical research.
2. **Start with a precise question** — define the question, gather relevant evidence, and make a decision explicit.
3. **Make the workflow visible** — connect question, method, evidence, and decision in one editable process diagram.

This is sample structure and illustrative copy, not a claim about a specific study.
